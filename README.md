\# ⚡ RazorBoost AI



> Turn conversations into revenue — safely.



RazorBoost AI is a bounded AI commerce agent built for the Razorpay Hackathon under \*\*AI Growth \& Agentic Commerce\*\*.



It helps merchants convert natural-language customer conversations into product discovery, recommendations, contextual upsells, cart creation, and Razorpay checkout — while ensuring that every money action is \*\*policy-controlled and explicitly approved by the customer\*\*.



\---



\## 🚀 The Problem



AI agents are becoming the new interface for commerce.



But merchants still face a major gap:



\- AI can recommend products, but often cannot complete commerce end-to-end.

\- AI-driven purchases need strong safety boundaries.

\- Merchants need higher conversion and average order value.

\- Every payment action needs to be explainable and auditable.



The key question is:



> \*\*How can an AI agent sell more without being allowed to spend money autonomously?\*\*



\---



\## 💡 Our Solution



\*\*RazorBoost AI\*\* acts as an intelligent sales assistant for merchants.



It understands customer intent, searches the merchant catalog, recommends relevant products, identifies contextual upsell opportunities, calculates the cart, validates the purchase against merchant policies, and creates a Razorpay test-mode order.



However:



> \*\*The AI can recommend and sell — but it cannot independently spend money.\*\*



Before any payment order is created, the customer must explicitly approve the purchase.
